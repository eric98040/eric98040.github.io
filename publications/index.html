Scalable Continuous Conditional Diffusion via Random Projections and Prioritized Sampling

Jaewon Kim*, Hyo-Seok Hwang*, Junhee Seok

* Equal contribution.

Continuous conditional generation beyond scalar labels.

Continuous conditional diffusion models usually condition on a single scalar label. HYPE-CCDM extends them to multi-dimensional regression labels. To handle distance concentration and label sparsity in high dimensions, we reformulate the vicinal objective with random 1D projections and add distance-aware prioritized sampling, achieving state-of-the-art results.

Fokker–Planck Soft Actor–Critic

Hyo-Seok Hwang, Jaewon Kim, Junhee Seok

Learning multi-modal policies without mode collapse.

We recast soft policy improvement as steering a policy toward the stationary distribution of a Langevin SDE, and derive a distribution-level objective from the corresponding Fokker–Planck equation. Training normalizing-flow policies by minimizing the PDE residual avoids the mode collapse of reverse-KL updates, improving performance and stability on multi-goal, MuJoCo, and Meta-World benchmarks.
